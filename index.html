Help Desk
How can I make a report?
Select a complaint category, describe the issue, optionally attach photos or videos, and publish your report.
Can I stay anonymous?
Yes. Select "Post anonymously" before publishing. Avoid adding personal information inside the report itself.
What should I avoid?
Avoid false accusations, threats, hate speech, private information and deliberately misleading content.
Media upload
You can select multiple image or video files. Large files may take longer to upload.

Anti-Corruption Awareness
