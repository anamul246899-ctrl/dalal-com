Help

Important information for using ঘুষ সাইট safely and responsibly.

How do I create a report?

Go to Home, write your experience in the report box and press Post. You can also attach photos or videos.

Can I post anonymously?

Yes. Turn on “Post anonymously” before submitting your report. Your profile name will not be displayed with that post.

What should I report?

You may share experiences involving bribery, middlemen, extortion, fraud, abuse of power, public-service harassment, job-related money demands and other corruption-related issues.

Important

Do not publish passwords, OTP codes, bank PINs, NID numbers, private addresses or other highly sensitive personal information.

Responsible reporting

Describe what you personally experienced and avoid unsupported accusations against identifiable people. Keep reports factual and respectful.
